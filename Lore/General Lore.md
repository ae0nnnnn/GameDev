# General Lore 

## The Balance and Its Properties

The Balance simply put is the energy/Entity that exists in all things, from the balance between electrons and protons (most times) in all matter, the Balance between day and night, The Balance between life and death etc. <span class="narrator">(I think you get the gist by now)</span> 

The Balance’s meta-physical entity is called Æon Bipolaris (their pure name) / Nihilomnia (what the mortals could understand it as) and out of a longing for purpose and companionship split their soul into three separate beings, This event was then recorded as the Divine Fracture in scripture.

Those three beings ended up being the Celestials; [[Asdeath]], Asmondaios and Lunaris
